Workflow Architecture

How MaintainX operates from report to resolution.

Four streamlined phases designed for zero friction and complete operational visibility.

PHASE 01
Report
Authenticated occupants log maintenance tickets in seconds, specifying location, category, urgency, and exact problem details.
PHASE 02
Assign
Maintenance teams inspect open requests, evaluate priority levels, and allocate specialized staff to address the fault.
PHASE 03
Track
Both users and management monitor status transitions live with clean status badges and timestamped record updates.
PHASE 04
Resolve
Work is marked complete, closing the loop, updating metrics, and archiving the record for historical auditability.
