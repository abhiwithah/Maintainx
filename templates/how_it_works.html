System Lifecycle

The standard protocol for maintenance resolution.

Step-by-step workflow designed to transform chaotic facility issues into organized, predictable, and measurable resolutions.

STEP 01
Submit Ticket
Occupants and staff log into MaintainX and submit an issue ticket detailing room location, symptom, priority, and maintenance category.
STEP 02
Queue & Triage
The request enters the central database with state Pending, ready for immediate review and prioritization by managers.
STEP 03
Dispatch & Repair
Technicians address the physical failure, transitioning the complaint to In Progress with clear progress milestones.
STEP 04
Verify & Resolve
Once confirmed functional, the request status is updated to Resolved, providing immediate confirmation to the submitter.
